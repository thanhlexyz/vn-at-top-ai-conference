## Figures and how they were computed

- ICLR: 61 submissions found on OpenReview, 13 accepted, 48 rejected or withdrawn.
- NeurIPS 2026: 33 accepted papers of the 26 people, 11 of them so far only on personal pages or announcements.
- Rejections behind them, low (60, exact 62.1): Sum over this year's NeurIPS papers of (ICLR submitted + 1) / (ICLR accepted + 1) - 1 for the people on the paper (averaged when several are), i.e. the website's per-person estimate. People without ICLR submissions add nothing.
- Rejections behind them, high (120, exact 121.8): NeurIPS 2026 papers x (ICLR submitted / ICLR accepted - 1), the group's pooled ICLR ratio.
- 41 of 79: Accepted papers with per-author affiliations on which no author other than the listed person(s) is at the listed person's institution.
- 42 of 79: Accepted papers with per-author affiliations whose last author is someone other than the listed person and is at an institution outside Vietnam (a company affiliation does not say which country).
- 64 of 79: Accepted papers with per-author affiliations with at least one author outside Vietnam.
- For balance (not in the Short): the first author is at the listed person's own institution on 35 of 79 papers, at another institution in Vietnam on 18, abroad on 25; most authors are from the person's institution on 25 of 79. Author lists do not say who is a student.
