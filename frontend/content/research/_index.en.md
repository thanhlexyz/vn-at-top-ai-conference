Here you can explore what AI researchers in Vietnam are working on. Choose a topic below to see who publishes in that area, ranked by their CSRankings score in that topic, or scroll through the list to read a short summary of each professor's work by topic. Clicking on a professor's name opens their page with a paper-by-paper breakdown: the problem they tackled, the main idea, what the paper reports, what the authors say remains open, and the questions raised in public peer reviews.

*These pages only summarize publicly available material, namely the papers themselves and their public reviews. Researchers who specialize in each area may weigh the strengths and weaknesses differently, as they have a closer view of where the field is heading and which problems are most promising.*
