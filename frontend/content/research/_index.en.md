Who works on what. Pick a topic to see which professors in Vietnam work on it, ranked by their CSRankings score in it, or scroll for each professor's summary by topic. A professor's page then goes through their papers: the problem, the idea, what the papers report, what the authors say is left open and the questions raised in public reviews.

*These pages only summarise public material: the papers and their public reviews. Experts in each field may see different strengths and weaknesses, since they know where the field is heading and which problems are worth pursuing.*
