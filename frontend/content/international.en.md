Each blue circle is a researcher abroad who has a profile on [VietProfs](https://vietprofs.roars.dev/), the directory of Vietnamese professors worldwide, and who is on more than four papers counted for the professors on the list (5 or more); each grey circle is a professor on the list. A line joins an author to a professor they share papers with, and the number on it, shown from 2 papers up, is how many. Authors are told apart by name and by the university printed on the papers. Click an author to open the profile on VietProfs, or a professor to open their page here.
