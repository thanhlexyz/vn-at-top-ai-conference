What each tracked professor works on, in plain words. Each name leads to a page that walks through their papers one by one: the problem, the idea, its strengths, what the authors themselves leave open, and the questions raised in the public reviews. The pages are written for students and researchers from any area of computer science, so field-specific terms are explained.

*These pages only summarise public material: the papers and their public reviews. Researchers in each field may see different strengths and weaknesses, since they know where the field is heading and which problems are worth pursuing.*
