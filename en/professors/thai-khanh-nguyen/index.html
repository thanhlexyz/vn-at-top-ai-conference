Thai Khanh Nguyen

Lecturer, Posts and Telecommunications Institute of Technology. Affiliated with a Vietnamese university since 2026.

Prof. Khanh's paper brings tools from control theory to the training of large language models. Work on a stability-based loss for language models that keeps hidden-state trajectories inside a bounded tube during fine-tuning, with no architectural change.

Open challenges. Work on trajectory regularization for language models leaves open guarantees beyond training trajectories, inference-time stability, and scaling past 8B parameters.
