Cong Tran

Assistant Professor, Dean, Faculty of Artificial Intelligence; Director, Center for Advanced Training in Artificial Intelligence, Posts and Telecommunications Institute of Technology. Affiliated with a Vietnamese university since 2021.

Prof. Cong's papers apply learning-based and graph-based methods to network data and images. Work on reinforcement-learning seed selection for spreading influence across multiplex networks, and on choosing which nodes to label in incomplete graphs. Work on graph neural networks refining image features to improve fine-grained classification of visually similar categories, such as bird species and dog breeds.

Open challenges. Work on seed selection for spreading influence leaves open unknown spreading patterns, scarce high-quality training samples, and active learning on graphs with missing links. Work on graph networks for fine-grained image recognition leaves open why a graph is needed and how results depend on batch size and sampling.
