Hoa Tien Nguyen

Assistant Professor, Hanoi University of Science and Technology. Affiliated with a Vietnamese university since 2022.

Prof. Hoa's listed work is an ICLR 2024 submission on optimizers for training neural networks with very large batches. Work on large-batch training without learning-rate warm-up, replacing warm-up in the LARS optimizer with a sigmoid-like schedule meant to avoid sharp minima.

Open challenges. Work on warm-up-free large-batch optimizers leaves open whether they scale to modern large datasets, how much tuning they need, and why early training is unstable.
