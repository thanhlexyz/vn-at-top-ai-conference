Hai Thanh Nguyen

Associate Professor, Vice Dean, Faculty of Information Systems, Can Tho University. Affiliated with a Vietnamese university since 2024.

Prof. Hai's paper combines a large language model with a graph neural network to diagnose diseases from doctor-patient conversations. Work on diagnosis from doctor-patient dialogues has an LLM extract symptoms, links patients sharing symptoms in a graph, and predicts diseases from neighbours.

Open challenges. Work on patient graphs built from symptoms leaves open whether one shared symptom makes graphs too dense and whether unchecked LLM extraction adds noise.
