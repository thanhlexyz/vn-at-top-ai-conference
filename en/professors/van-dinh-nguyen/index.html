Van-Dinh Nguyen

Assistant Professor, VinUniversity. Affiliated with a Vietnamese university since 2022.

Work on communication-efficient federated learning and lightweight WiFi-based sensing for resource-limited settings. Work on communication-efficient hierarchical federated learning that exchanges compact feature statistics between tiers instead of per-sample activations and gradients. Work on human pose estimation from WiFi signals with a lightweight convolutional network, as a lower-cost alternative to camera-based sensing.

Open challenges. Work on communication-efficient hierarchical federated learning leaves open richer feature distributions than diagonal Gaussians and topology-aware client assignment. Work on human pose estimation from WiFi signals leaves open robustness to noise, interference and multipath propagation in the signals.
