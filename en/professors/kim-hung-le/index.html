Kim-Hung Le

Lecturer, University of Information Technology, VNU-HCM. Affiliated with a Vietnamese university since 2019.

Prof. Hung's work builds on classical discriminant analysis instead of deep networks. Work on tuning-free anomaly detection on tables uses a closed-form discriminant method trained on normal data, with one fixed configuration for every dataset.

Open challenges. Work on tuning-free anomaly detection on tables leaves open training cost for many features, nonlinear data, and normal data made of several sub-distributions.
