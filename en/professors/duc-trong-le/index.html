Duc-Trong Le

Assistant Professor, Head, Department of Computer Science, VNU University of Engineering and Technology. Affiliated with a Vietnamese university since 2019.

Multimodal and graph-based deep learning for emotion recognition, image classification and continual learning. Work on multimodal learning under missing modalities and on multi-label image classification that models label dependencies with graph transformers. Work on parameter-efficient continual learning that combines Fourier-domain adapters with hierarchical memory to reduce forgetting without storing past exemplars. Work on multimodal emotion recognition in conversations with graph neural networks, combining utterance-level temporal dependencies and cross-modal interactions.

Open challenges. Work on balanced multimodal learning leaves broader multimodal tasks and large-scale pretrained backbones for future study. Work on exemplar-free continual learning still faces no guaranteed retention of past predictions, modest gains from some components, and sensitivity to many design choices. Work on conversational emotion recognition is limited by incomplete hyperparameter tuning of context windows and attention heads under limited computational resources.
