Ngoc-Thanh Dinh

Associate Professor, Director, AI Practice Program, VinUniversity. Affiliated with a Vietnamese university since 2026.

Prof. Thanh's recent papers develop training objectives that align generative models with human judgments. Work on aligning text-to-image diffusion models with human preferences through an offline objective that weights preference pairs and keeps the model's denoising ability. Work on safety alignment as density ratio matching derives single-stage preference losses from a closed-form likelihood ratio of the optimal safe policy.

Open challenges. Work on preference alignment of diffusion models leaves open how much the ratio objective contributes and how image-level preferences relate to per-step learning. Work on safety alignment of language models leaves open results beyond one safety dataset, small backbones and binary safety labels.
