Thinh Pham

Assistant Professor, VinUniversity. Affiliated with a Vietnamese university since 2025.

Prof. Thinh's paper studies how to evaluate language models before they are used in safety-critical operations. Work on consequence-aware evaluation of language models uses air traffic control to measure how far standard semantic scores overstate operational reliability.

Open challenges. Work on safety-focused evaluation of language models leaves open how to close the gap between semantic scores and operational safety, which risk-aware fine-tuning only narrows.
