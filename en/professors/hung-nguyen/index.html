Hung Nguyen

Senior Lecturer, Dean, Faculty of Information Technology; Head, Department of Computer Science, Ho Chi Minh City University of Education. Affiliated with a Vietnamese university since 2020.

Prof. Hung's paper regularizes polynomial models so that more complex terms are always penalized more. Work on ordered penalties for polynomial regression designs convex penalties under which higher-degree terms always cost more than lower-degree ones, even near zero.

Open challenges. Work on ordered penalties for polynomial regression leaves open when degree-ordered nonlinear penalties beat degree-weighted lasso, and whether they give simpler or more stable models.
