Huynh Thi Thanh Binh

Professor, Vice Dean, School of Information and Communication Technology, Hanoi University of Science and Technology.

Most of Prof. Binh's papers use large language models or learned policies to automate design and decision tasks. Work on LLM-guided heuristic evolution for NP-hard combinatorial problems with diversity control, multi-turn reflection, multi-objective selection and two-agent turn-taking. Work on reinforcement learning under drifting preferences that infers them from history, and on multi-agent reinforcement learning coordinating LLM agents to write related-work sections. Work on simulated crowds for robot navigation and on synthesizing one million grasp samples with foundation models.

Open challenges. Work on multi-turn LLM heuristic refinement leaves open whether it helps beyond bin packing, which parts matter, its computation cost, and balancing exploration with exploitation. Work on constrained reinforcement learning under drift leaves open long-horizon budgets, simple evaluation environments, and coordination beyond a fixed action set and one benchmark. Work on synthetic grasp data from foundation models leaves diversity dependent on those models' knowledge of everyday objects, and simulated crowds on one dynamics model.
