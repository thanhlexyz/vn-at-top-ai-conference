Hien D. Nguyen

Associate Professor, University of Information Technology, VNU-HCM. Affiliated with a Vietnamese university since 2025.

Prof. Hien D. Nguyen's paper designs convex regularizers that encode a preference for simpler polynomial terms. Work on ordered penalties for polynomial regression designs convex penalties under which higher-degree terms always cost more than lower-degree ones, even near zero.

Open challenges. Work on ordered penalties for polynomial regression leaves open when degree-ordered nonlinear penalties beat degree-weighted lasso, and whether they give simpler or more stable models.
