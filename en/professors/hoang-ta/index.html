Hoang Ta

Lecturer, Hanoi University of Science and Technology. Affiliated with a Vietnamese university since 2025.

Prof. Hoang's papers study how to spend limited samples so that decisions in stochastic environments are provably reliable. Work on best-arm identification with one-bit feedback and variance-driven sampling gives guarantees for selecting the best option under limited information. Work on graph-based Monte-Carlo planning that shares states reached at the same depth across trajectories, making planning in stochastic environments more sample efficient.

Open challenges. Work on best-arm identification with one-bit feedback leaves open removing the known anchor-point assumption near each arm's mean and the effect of first-order variance approximation. Work on graph-based Monte-Carlo planning leaves open the runtime and memory overhead of sharing states, and how it compares with broader reinforcement learning baselines.
