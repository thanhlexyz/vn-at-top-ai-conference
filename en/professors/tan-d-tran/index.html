Tan D. Tran

Lecturer, Phenikaa University.

Approximation algorithms with provable guarantees for submodular maximization under cardinality, knapsack and matroid constraints. Work on parallel algorithms with linear queries for non-monotone submodular maximization, fair k-submodular maximization under matroids and knapsack-constrained submodular maximization.

Open challenges. Work on fair k-submodular maximization with open richer constraint settings and parallel linear-query algorithms with open proof rigor and per-metric gains.
