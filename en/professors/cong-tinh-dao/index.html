Cong-Tinh Dao

Lecturer, Can Tho University. Affiliated with a Vietnamese university since 2024.

Prof. Cong-Tinh Dao's work combines large language models with graph neural networks for medical applications. Work on diagnosis from doctor-patient dialogues has an LLM extract symptoms, links patients sharing symptoms in a graph, and predicts diseases from neighbours.

Open challenges. Work on patient graphs built from symptoms leaves open whether one shared symptom makes graphs too dense and whether unchecked LLM extraction adds noise.
