Minh Nhat Vu

Assistant Professor, VinUniversity. Affiliated with a Vietnamese university since 2025.

Prof. Minh's papers study how robots can model, decide and act reliably under uncertainty or with little data. Work on few-shot adaptation of vision-language-action models through future-embedding conditioning, stopping rules for probing articulated objects and uncertainty-weighted physics-informed dynamics models.

Open challenges. Work on few-shot robot adaptation with open grounding-model failures, articulated-object probing with open real-robot evaluation and physics-informed dynamics with open real-time cost.
