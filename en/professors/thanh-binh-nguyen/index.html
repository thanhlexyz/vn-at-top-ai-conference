Thanh-Binh Nguyen

Associate Professor, Head, Department of Applied Informatics, University of Science, VNU-HCM. Affiliated with a Vietnamese university since 2012.

Machine learning methods for efficient models, language model alignment, explanation, forecasting, federated learning and medical imaging. Work on aligning language models from offline preference data with importance sampling, and on retrieval-augmented compliance checking of Vietnamese medical advertisements. Work on server-trained generators of synthetic data that reduce catastrophic forgetting in federated class-incremental learning, plus fractional neural ODEs for trajectory forecasting. Work on domain generalization in hierarchical federated learning, token merging in Transformers and hypernetwork routing in sparse mixtures of experts.

Open challenges. Work on preference-based alignment leaves open the clipping threshold and preference-data assumptions; compliance checking leaves open semantic and logical reasoning errors. Work on generators for federated class-incremental learning leaves open client storage burden and the training cost of two generators, and what learned fractional orders capture. Work on hierarchical federated generalization with open model adaptation, token merging with open higher merge ratios and hypernetwork routing with open cross-layer sharing.
