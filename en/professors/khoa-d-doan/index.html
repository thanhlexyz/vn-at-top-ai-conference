Khoa D. Doan

Assistant Professor, Associate Director, VinUni-Illinois Smart Health Center, VinUniversity. Affiliated with a Vietnamese university since 2022.

Work centers on the security of deep learning models, mainly backdoor and adversarial attacks and defenses, alongside language-model training and federated learning. Work on backdoor attacks, including clean-label backdoors from target-class data only, and randomized and patch-based defenses across image, language, federated, molecular and 3D-scene settings. Work on aligning language models with preference optimization and verifiable-reward reinforcement learning, and on reasoning-model behavior. Work on attributing concepts to model parameters in text-to-image diffusion models, then editing them at inference time to erase or amplify a concept.

Open challenges. Work on backdoor attacks and defenses leaves open evaluation against only older defense strategies, retry attacks on randomized defenses, and countermeasures still to be built. Work on reward over-optimization leaves open how to set key hyper-parameters, assumptions about preference data, and generalization beyond mathematics. Work on parameter-level concept editing leaves open how well it generalizes beyond early diffusion models and how cleanly it removes a concept without side effects.
