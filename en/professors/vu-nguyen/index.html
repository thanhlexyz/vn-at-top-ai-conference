Vu Nguyen

Associate Professor, Vice Dean, Faculty of Information Technology; Head, Department of Software Engineering, University of Science, VNU-HCM. Affiliated with a Vietnamese university since 2022.

Prof. Vu's papers examine when conclusions from machine-learning evaluations are actually supported by the evidence. Work on auditing modality-importance claims from attribution scores, using statistical inference across several plausible ways of removing a modality. Work on evaluating endpoints of asymmetric training in multi-agent reinforcement learning by resuming all learning and comparing with continued co-adaptation.

Open challenges. Work on auditing modality-importance claims leaves open results beyond one dataset and predictor, and effects such as feature scale and covariance. Work on evaluating endpoints of asymmetric training leaves open comparison with prior methods, generality beyond one toy market-making testbed, and richer market mechanisms.
