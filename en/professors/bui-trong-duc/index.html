Bui Trong Duc

Lecturer, Hanoi University of Science and Technology. Affiliated with a Vietnamese university since 2025.

Prof. Duc's recent papers use reinforcement learning to make decisions adapt to signals that fixed rules handle poorly. Work on reinforcement learning under drifting preferences learns policies that trade off several rewards when preferences and safety budgets drift or are noisy. Work on learned retrieval of optimization templates for LLM-written solver code, trained by reinforcement learning from solver and judge feedback.

Open challenges. Work on constrained reinforcement learning under drift leaves open long-horizon resource depletion and evaluation on environments beyond simple ones. Work on template retrieval for LLM solver code leaves open whether gains come from the learned policy or from a hand-built template graph.
