Cuong Viet Ta

Lecturer with PhD, VNU University of Engineering and Technology.

Graph representation learning on curved manifolds, plus hierarchical discrete representations. Work on graph models on curved manifolds, working directly on the manifold for node classification and link prediction on static and dynamic graphs. Work on hierarchical discrete latent representations that go beyond flat latents, analysed through sample complexity and codebook utilization.

Open challenges. Work on selecting and scaling curved-space graph models remains open: manifold search is heuristic and evaluation covers mostly small graphs. Work on hierarchical discrete latent spaces is covered only through sample complexity and codebook utilization in the available material.
