Son Van Nguyen

Lecturer, Phenikaa University. Affiliated with a Vietnamese university since 2024.

Prof. Son's two listed papers combine large language models with evolutionary algorithms. Work on LLM-guided heuristic evolution lets an LLM refine groups of related heuristics for combinatorial optimization over several turns, using performance feedback.

Open challenges. Work on multi-turn LLM heuristic refinement leaves open whether it helps beyond online bin packing, which of its parts matter, and what its computation costs.
