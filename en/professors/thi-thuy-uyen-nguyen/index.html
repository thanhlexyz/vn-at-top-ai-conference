Thi Thuy Uyen Nguyen

Lecturer, Hue University. Affiliated with a Vietnamese university since 2014.

Prof. Uyen's recent paper combines pretrained foundation models for medical image analysis when labeled data is scarce. Work on fusing pathology image-text models for few-shot classification, which aligns the frozen models' embeddings and jointly refines image features and class prototypes.

Open challenges. Work on fusing pathology image-text models leaves open whether fusion beats a plain ensemble of separately adapted models, and its computation and memory cost.
