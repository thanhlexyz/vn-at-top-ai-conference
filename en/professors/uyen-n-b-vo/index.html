Uyen N.B. Vo

Lecturer, Posts and Telecommunications Institute of Technology. Affiliated with a Vietnamese university since 2025.

Prof. Uyen's recent papers bring stability theory and matrix conditioning into the training of sequence models. Work on state space models for sequences adds a momentum pathway and spectrally conditioned updates to keep memory stable over long sequences. Work on a stability-based loss for language models that keeps hidden-state trajectories inside a bounded tube during fine-tuning, with no architectural change.

Open challenges. Work on sequence modelling with state space models leaves open results at larger scale, hyperparameter selection and direct validation of the rank-enrichment analysis. Work on trajectory regularization for language models leaves open guarantees beyond training trajectories, inference-time stability, and scaling past 8B parameters.
