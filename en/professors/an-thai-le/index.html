An Thai Le

Assistant Professor, VinUniversity. Affiliated with a Vietnamese university since 2025.

Robot learning and motion planning: few-shot vision-language-action adaptation and multi-robot planning under temporal-logic tasks. Work on few-shot adaptation of vision-language-action models through future-embedding conditioning and GPU-parallel multi-robot motion planning under temporal-logic tasks.

Open challenges. Work on few-shot robot adaptation with open grounding-model failures and multi-robot planning with open human interaction.
