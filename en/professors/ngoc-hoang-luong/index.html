Ngoc Hoang Luong

Lecturer, University of Information Technology, VNU-HCM. Affiliated with a Vietnamese university since 2019.

Search-based optimization applied to neural architecture search and to adversarial attacks on dialogue models. Work on black-box attacks on dialogue models crafts adversarial prompts that lower response coherence and lengthen outputs, using only inputs and outputs. Work on automated zero-cost proxy design combines existing hand-crafted proxies through symbolic regression to rank neural architectures without training. Work on fitness landscapes of zero-cost proxies uses local optima networks to examine proxy-guided local search for neural architectures.

Open challenges. Work on black-box attacks on dialogue models still faces high computational cost and evaluation limited to specific datasets and older models. Work on automated zero-cost proxy design still lacks explanation of why metric combinations work and is outperformed by some existing methods in downstream search. Work on fitness landscapes of zero-cost proxies is shown on few small benchmarks and local search only; other optimizers are not covered.
