Hong T.M. Chu

Assistant Professor, VinUniversity. Affiliated with a Vietnamese university since 2024.

Prof. Hong's paper studies how to measure the robustness of deep neural networks when the whole data distribution, not a single input, is perturbed. Work on tighter robustness certificates for deep networks under Wasserstein distributional robustness, plus attacks that construct worst-case distributions rather than point-wise perturbations.

Open challenges. Work on Wasserstein robustness certificates leaves open performance on transformer models and ImageNet, attacks beyond white-box access, and a clearer practical benefit of tight bounds.
