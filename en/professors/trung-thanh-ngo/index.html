Trung Thanh Ngo

Lecturer, Hanoi University of Science and Technology. Affiliated with a Vietnamese university since 2022.

Prof. Trung works on recovering the 3D shape and material properties of objects from images. Work on inverse rendering of translucent objects estimates shape and light scattering inside the material from very few images, using flash photos or polarized light.

Open challenges. Work on inverse rendering of translucent objects leaves open scattering that varies across the object and capture without a flash and no-flash image pair.
