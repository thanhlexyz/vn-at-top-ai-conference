Minh Do

Professor, Dean, College of Engineering and Computer Science, VinUniversity.

Prof. Minh's papers study representation learning for multimodal and image data, together with ways to generate or compress the training data itself. Work on multimodal pretraining with missing modalities and robust multimodal learning with limited labels. Work on closed-form contrastive alignment replaces repeated minibatch gradient training with one spectral decomposition, for linear and kernelized encoders. Work on condensing gigapixel pathology slides into a slide-adaptive set of concept prototypes by decomposing each slide, condensing, and pruning unused concepts.

Open challenges. Work on missing-modality reconstruction with open uncertainty benefits and absence-pattern coverage. Work on closed-form contrastive alignment leaves open whether it scales to large projection heads, full encoder fine-tuning and datasets far larger than those tested. Work on condensing gigapixel pathology slides leaves open the gap to full-slide features and the reliance on public datasets with site-specific staining artifacts.
