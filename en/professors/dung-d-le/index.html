Dung D. Le

Assistant Professor, Director, Data Science Program, VinUniversity. Affiliated with a Vietnamese university since 2021.

Efficient and robust training methods and multimodal learning, plus federated settings, unlearning and dialogue planning. Work on adapting CLIP to long captions, on few-shot classification with synthetic images, and on object-level out-of-distribution detection with generative models. Work on meta pseudo-labeling without second-order gradients, memory-efficient continual learning, and attention architectures with fewer parameters. Work on removing specified training data from models, in mixture-of-experts and federated settings, and on predicting adversarial robustness.

Open challenges. Work on image-text alignment in CLIP that is evaluated mainly on retrieval, at small training scale and with limited comparison to region-based methods. Work on semi-supervised and continual learning where analysis covers idealized settings and reported gains remain small or weakly demonstrated. Work on unlearning by design that needs deletion groups known at training time and may degrade with very many clients.
