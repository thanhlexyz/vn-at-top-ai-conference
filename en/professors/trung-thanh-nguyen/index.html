Trung Thanh Nguyen

Associate Professor, Vice Dean, Faculty of Data Science and Artificial Intelligence; Head of the DataOpt Lab, National Economics University. Affiliated with a Vietnamese university since 2020.

Prof. Thanh's paper gives polynomial-time approximation algorithms for a computationally hard fair-division problem. Work on fair and efficient allocation of goods approximates the trade-off between max-min fairness and total utility when indivisible items are divided among agents.

Open challenges. Work on fair and efficient allocation of goods gives polynomial-time guarantees only with few item types or few agent types; other fairness notions are untested.
