Trinh Van Chien

Assistant Professor, Head of the Computer Networks and Next-Generation Communication Technology Lab, Hanoi University of Science and Technology.

Prof. Chien's recent paper studies personalized federated learning, where clients with differing data train partly shared models. Work on layer-wise personalized federated learning decides which layers clients share and which they personalize by measuring conflicts between client gradients in each layer.

Open challenges. Work on layer-wise personalized federated learning leaves open how to choose the personalized layers optimally and how gradient angles affect collaboration.
