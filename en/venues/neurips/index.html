NeurIPS: Conference on Neural Information Processing Systems

NeurIPS publishes accepted papers. A rejected paper is public only when its authors chose to make it so, which means the rejected numbers below are a lower bound and submitted numbers cannot be given. The numbers cover the listed professors; a paper with several of them is counted once. 22 accepted before 2026, 32 from 2026. 32 accepted papers are known only from personal pages. They are shown as +32, tagged "unofficial" in the list, and stay so until the conference publishes them.

NeurIPS does not publish its rejections, so they are estimated from the ICLR record of each paper's listed authors, as on their pages: about 3.0 submissions per accepted paper (see About).
