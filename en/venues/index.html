ICLR is the only venue that makes every submission public. There, the listed professors have an acceptance rate of 16%, roughly half the 30% that ICLR reports across all submissions. At the other ten venues, by contrast, the rates estimated here (25–41%) match or exceed those the conferences report (14–27%). There are two possibilities: one, the listed professors do better at the other conferences than at ICLR; two, this site heavily undercounts, not only the rejected papers but the total number of submissions as well.
