Research directions: Quan Minh Phan

Quan Minh Phan (UIT) · All professors

Work on zero-cost proxies for neural architecture search: automated design and proxy-guided local search. Work on automated zero-cost proxy design combines existing hand-crafted proxies through symbolic regression to rank neural architectures without training. Work on fitness landscapes of zero-cost proxies uses local optima networks to examine proxy-guided local search for neural architectures.

Open challenges. Work on automated zero-cost proxy design still lacks explanation of why metric combinations work and is outperformed by some existing methods in downstream search. Work on fitness landscapes of zero-cost proxies is shown on few small benchmarks and local search only; other optimizers are not covered.

Deep learning and representation learning (1 paper · CSRankings score 0.50)

Topic description

This paper addresses the inconsistency of zero-cost proxies, which estimate network performance without training, and the manual effort of designing them. A symbolic regression framework synthesizes new proxies from existing hand-crafted ones. Experiments on 13 problems in a NAS benchmark suite report a strong positive rank correlation with network performance, with a proxy produced in a few minutes.

Papers (1)
Crafting Zero-Cost Proxy Metrics for Neural Architecture Search via Symbolic Regression ICLR 2025 · rejected
The problem
Zero-cost (ZC) proxies estimate network performance in neural architecture search (NAS) without training, but their performance varies across problems and they are designed manually through trial and error.
The idea
A framework based on symbolic regression that automatically combines existing hand-crafted ZC metrics into a new ZC metric.
Strengths
Reports a metric with strong positive rank correlation to network performance across 13 problems in NAS-Bench-Suite-Zero, produced within a few minutes.
Questions raised by reviewers
  • The paper focuses on the performance of the designed ZC metrics but does not provide insights into why certain combinations of metrics work better than others.
  • I think testing on Imagenet using the DARTS search space is necessary.
  • The proposed ZC metric, while using existing ZC metrics, doesn't outright outperform these metrics (see Fig. 6), and in NAS, is outperformed by a number of existing NAS methods based on ZC metrics (see Table 4)
  • While the paper claims generalizability across various NAS problems, the evaluation is primarily based on a limited set of benchmarks.

Optimization methods (1 paper · CSRankings score 0.50)

Topic description

This paper argues that relying only on zero-cost proxies appears less effective than training-based metrics such as validation accuracy. It builds fitness landscapes of proxy-based local searches with local optima networks and reports that a high rank correlation does not guarantee finding high-performance architectures. Reviews describe a two-stage method of proxy-guided local search followed by successive halving.

Papers (1)
Efficient Multi-Fidelity NAS with Zero-Cost Proxy-Guided Local Search ICLR 2024 · rejected
The problem
Using zero-cost proxies alone in NAS appears less effective than training-based metrics such as validation accuracy, and rank correlation with test accuracy may not predict how well a proxy guides search.
The idea
Constructs fitness landscapes of proxy-based local searches using Local Optima Networks, then proposes a two-stage method: proxy-guided local search to shrink the search space, followed by successive halving (a multi-fidelity method) with training-based evaluation.
Strengths
Reports that a high correlation does not guarantee finding high-performance architectures; reviewers' summaries describe evaluation on NAS-Bench-101, NAS-Bench-201 and NAS-Bench-ASR with better results than other black-box optimizers.
Questions raised by reviewers
  • The authors did evaluate their algorithm only on 3 tabular benchmarks, while there are many more available in NAS-Bench-Suite-Zero.
  • On the one hand, the search space is quite small (NAS-Bench-201); On the other hand, only local search algorithm is explored, the results and conclusion may not suitable for EA or BO.
  • To claim the limitation of ZC metrics, more theoretical analysis is required.
  • both the preliminary analysis and performance comparison lack of the study of the recent methods of this area.
