Topic description

Financial forecasting needs long-range temporal modelling at linear cost, adaptive cross-asset structure and calibrated uncertainty. The framework combines a bidirectional Mamba encoder with a multi-head adaptive graph layer using Chebyshev spectral filtering, and obtains predictive variances by treating adjacencies and filters as random through Monte Carlo Dropout and DropEdge. It reports gains in point accuracy and calibration over established baselines on NASDAQ, NYSE and DJIA data.

Integrating Selective State-Space Models and Bayesian Graph Attention for Uncertainty-aware Time-Series Analysis ICLR 2026 · rejected
The problem
Financial forecasting must capture long-range temporal dependencies across volatile market regimes at linear cost and learn adaptive relations between assets, while giving calibrated uncertainty for its predictions.
The idea
BIMAMBA encodes sequences in both directions with selective state-space models (Mamba), and MAGAC (Multi-head Adaptive Graph Attention Convolution) builds dynamic adjacencies by blending a Gaussian kernel with attention before Chebyshev spectral filtering; the Bayesian extension treats adjacencies and filters as random via Monte Carlo Dropout and DropEdge and propagates the predictive variance in closed form.
Strengths
The abstract reports improvements in both point prediction accuracy and uncertainty calibration over established baselines on U.S. equity indices (NASDAQ, NYSE and DJIA, per the reviews), with variance propagation at O(N) complexity.
What the authors leave open
  • The Bayesian components add training and inference overhead; lighter approximate inference such as small ensembles, variational Bayesian layers or structured dropout could retain most of the gains at lower cost, which the authors leave to future work.
Questions raised by reviewers
  • The reported IC values are extraordinarily high for daily single-day returns (e.g., IC=0.9413 on NASDAQ).
  • There is no ablation disentangling the gains from bidirectionality, Gaussian+attention blend, Chebyshev order, number of heads, Bayesian sampling, or DropEdge.
  • Table-reported MACs are for S=1 while the method elsewhere enables S=10 at inference; the “no overhead” claim is therefore misleading.
  • the description in Section 3.2.1 appears to describe standard attention mechanisms, not specifically graph attention.
