Research directions: Nguyen Duc Minh

Nguyen Duc Minh (DHTL) · All professors

Prof. Minh's paper studies why video-language models make errors about time and proposes a fix without retraining. Work on training-free fixes for video language models that strengthen foreground tokens and frame-to-frame differences to reduce event-order and motion errors.

Open challenges. Work on video language model temporal errors leaves open which part of the fix produces the gains and how the model uses temporal information.

Computer vision and multimodal learning (1 paper · CSRankings score 0.17)

Topic description

Video large language models often answer questions about event order, motion direction and object-state change incorrectly. The paper reports that motion information is partly decodable from object-level visual features but not used in the final prediction, and proposes a training-free method that reweights foreground tokens and injects inter-frame feature differences. It reports higher accuracy than the unmodified models on three backbones.

Papers (1)
Training-Free Positive Spatiotemporal Enhancement for Mitigating Temporal Hallucinations in Video-LLMs ICLR 2027 · rejected
The problem
Video large language models (Video-LLMs) recognise objects and scenes reliably but misread event order, motion direction and object-state changes, producing temporal hallucinations; existing training-free fixes boost per-frame object evidence but not how the same object changes across frames.
The idea
Feature Enhance is a training-free method that modifies the visual tokens before the frozen language model: it up-weights foreground regions that persist across frames and injects foreground-gated differences between features of adjacent frames, without changing model parameters.
Strengths
Across LLaVA-OneVision, Qwen2.5-VL and LLaVA-Video, the paper reports higher average accuracy than the unmodified models, and a reviewer notes gains in all six backbone-benchmark combinations, up to +4.67 percentage points on VideoHallucer and +4.00 on EventHallusion.
What the authors leave open
  • The authors plan to explore motion-aligned correspondence, adaptive frame sampling and causal interventions to better characterise and improve how Video-LLMs use temporal information.
Questions raised by reviewers
  • In Table 2, the gains mainly come from non-temporal categories (e.g., SDH up to +8.50), while TPH gains only 1.71–3.87.
  • The diagnostic evidence does not sufficiently establish the proposed failure mechanism.
  • But the existing ablations do not isolate the contribution of the temporal residual.
  • the parameters of Feature Enhance are from testing 109 different configurations, and the evaluation results on TPH will be unreliable if Feature Enhance is tuned on evaluation data.
