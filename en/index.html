How the score is calculated. It is the adjusted count that CSRankings uses: each accepted paper is worth 1 divided by its number of authors to every listed professor who is an author of it, and a professor's score is the sum over their papers. A paper with 4 authors adds 0.25; a paper with 10 adds 0.1. An institution's score adds up its professors' scores in each of the four areas (AI, ML, CV, NLP) and takes the geometric mean of those four numbers, each plus 1, as CSRankings does. Official and unofficial papers both count, rejected papers do not. See About.
