Conference papers by professors and lecturers in Vietnam, 2020–2026

How many papers each professor submitted to ICLR, and how many were accepted at ICLR, NeurIPS, ICML, CVPR and ACL. Papers count from the year the professor joined a Vietnamese institution. Only ICLR publishes every submission, so only ICLR has submitted and rejected numbers.

All data on this site comes from public sources: OpenReview, the conferences’ own lists of accepted papers, CVF Open Access, the ACL Anthology, and the professors’ own web pages and lab announcements. Nothing private is used. The site only brings together records that anyone can already look up, and the papers link to their public sources. If you notice anything that is not correct, we would be grateful to hear about it on the Feedback page.

ICLR rejected counts rejected, withdrawn and desk-rejected submissions. Total rejected adds every ICLR rejection to the rejections authors made public at other venues (some NeurIPS papers), so it is a lower bound. Total rejected, estimated (orange) adds an estimate for the venues that hide their rejections, from each professor's own ICLR record: submissions per accepted paper = (ICLR submitted + 1) / (ICLR accepted + 1). It is an estimate, not a count. – means the OpenReview records of that professor have not been fetched. Main conference track only; workshops, Findings, position papers and the NeurIPS Datasets & Benchmarks track are left out. Professors are ordered by total accepted papers.

The official NeurIPS 2026 list was published before the conference and is not complete yet. For that list only, papers that a professor's own page lists are counted as well. They are shown as +N after the official number and are unofficial until the conference publishes them.

The co-author and topic columns are taken over all counted papers, accepted or not. Foreign means the affiliation printed on the paper is outside Vietnam; other institutions counts the different co-authors whose affiliation is not the professor's own institution. Both need a paper list with affiliations, and topic areas need the conference's own topic labels, which exist for ICLR, NeurIPS and ICML from 2023 or 2024 on. Each professor's page says how many papers a figure is based on.
