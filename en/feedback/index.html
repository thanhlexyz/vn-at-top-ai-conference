Feedback

If you notice a figure that is not correct, a paper that is missing or credited to the wrong person, or someone who should be on the list, we would be very grateful if you could let us know using the form below. Every piece of feedback is read with care. A link to the paper (OpenReview or the conference page) or to the relevant staff page helps us check and correct the record quickly.

After filling in the form, choose one of two ways to send it:

  • Continue on GitHub opens a new public issue with your message filled in; nothing is sent until you press “Create” there, and a GitHub account is needed.
  • Send by e-mail opens your own e-mail app with a message to hi@thanhle.xyz filled in; nothing is sent until you press send, and no account is needed.

Thank you very much for your time and help.

Feedback on GitHub is public, so we kindly ask you not to include private contact details there; you are also welcome to read the feedback already received. Feedback by e-mail goes only to the site maintainer.
